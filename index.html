Design Rationale

For this assignment, we used A-Frame and JavaScript libraries and W3Schools tutorials, to build a small 3D forest scene. The 3D scene was built with Blender, the other assets come from Pexels and SketchFab.

How we built it

The forest and the frog are 3D models (.glb files) loaded into an A-Frame scene, with a 360° image as the sky. The mouse acts as a pointer in the 3D world, so the frog can be clicked like a button. When you hover over the frog it grows slightly to show it can be clicked, and when you click it, an A-Frame animation makes it hop.

The game uses simple JavaScript. Pressing Start resets the score and starts a 10-second countdown with setInterval(). Each click on the frog is detected with addEventListener("click") and adds 1 to the jump counter. When the timer reaches 0, clearInterval() stops the countdown and your final score is shown. This popup is a modal, based on the W3Schools Modal Popup project.


How to play

Press Start to begin the game. Then click on the frog as many times as you can in 10 seconds! Your score will be displayed at the end of the game.

Don't forget to click start before clicking the frog!
